# 🚨 Recurring Mistake & Strike Tracker

> **The Strike Rule**:
> - Any repeated weak habit or grammar slip increments the strike counter (`+1 Strike`).
> - At **3x Strikes 🚨**, an immediate 5-second challenge is triggered in chat to correct it.
> - Using the correct pro form 3 times consecutively converts it to **Mastered ✅**.

| Mistake Pattern / Habit | Category | Total Strikes | The Pro Correction & Rule | Alert Level | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lowercase `i` & Lowercase Sentence Starts** (`i want`, `good but`) | Capitalization | `5x+` 🚨 | Capitalize **`I`** everywhere; capitalize the first letter of every sentence. | **Critical** | Drilling 🥊 |
| **Run-on Sentences** (40-70 words without periods) | Writing Flow | `4x` 🚨 | Break complex thoughts into 2-3 short, punchy sentences (10-15 words). | **Critical** | Drilling 🥊 |
| **Punctuation Spacing & Trailing Marks** (`word .`, trailing commas `,`) | Formatting | `3x` 🚨 | Attach punctuation directly to the preceding word (`word.`), no trailing commas. | **Critical** | Drilling 🥊 |
| **`it's` vs `its`** (Confusing verb with possessive) | Grammar | `2x` ⚠️ | `its` = possessive pronoun (`its full potential`). `it's` = contraction of *it is*. | High | Active Watch |
| **Weak Verb Prepositions** (`search about the market`, `in the icon`) | Prepositions | `2x` ⚠️ | `conduct market scans / research` (not search about), `under the settings icon`. | High | Active Watch |
| **Tech Process Verbs** (`is working` for services) | Tech Vocab | `2x` ⚠️ | Processes/services: use `is running`. Reserve `is working` for non-broken state. | High | Active Watch |
| **Missing Auxiliary in Questions** (`Why [subject] not working?`) | Grammar | `2x` ⚠️ | Always invert the auxiliary: `Why is the terminal...`, `Does it consume...?`. | High | Active Watch |
| **Informal Checking** (`did you get what i mean?`) | Conversation | `1x` | Use standard engineering phrases: `Does that make sense?`. | Normal | Watch |
| **Accountability Tone** (`you forgot to`) | Workplace | `1x` | Professional collaboration: `You missed [X]` or `We haven't updated [X] yet`. | Normal | Watch |
| **Spelling Confusions** (`grammer`, `calender`, `even thought`, `memize`) | Spelling | `1x each` | `grammar`, `calendar`, `even though`, `memorize`, `credits`. | Normal | Watch |
