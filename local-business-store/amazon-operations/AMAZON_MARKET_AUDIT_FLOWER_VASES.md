## 2. Customer Pain Points & Market Gaps (The 5 Vulnerabilities)

Based on qualitative analysis of 1-star and 2-star reviews of the category bestsellers:

1. **Shipping Breakage (High Transit Damage)**:
   - Packaging fails to cushion ceramic/glass/gypsum products.
   - Multiple reviews report items arriving smashed inside standard cardboard boxes.
2. **Material Fraud (Gypsum Substituted for Ceramic)**:
   - Products listed as "Ceramic" arrive as cheap chalky plaster/gypsum that chips and crumbles easily.
3. **Wobbly / Uneven Base**:
   - Bottom sanding is missing, causing vases to rock or topple over when flowers are inserted.
4. **Color & Texture Distortion**:
   - Delivered colors look stark chalky white rather than the advertised premium off-white/matte finish.
5. **The "Empty Shell" Disappointment**:
   - Buyers complain that vases arrive empty without flowers. Customers expect a complete decorative centerpiece ready to display.
