### Rating Taxonomy
- **[VERIFIED]**: The claim matches the physical product in hand, verified by photographic evidence or physical measurements.
- **[UNVERIFIED]**: The claim is plausible but lacks exact lab/workshop measurement or supplier certification.
- **[UNSAFE TO PUBLISH]**: The claim exposes the seller to customer returns, 1-star negative reviews, or Amazon listing suppression due to material misrepresentation or operational failure.

### Bullet 3: Washability & Cleaning (CRITICAL RISK)
> **Draft Text**:
> `EASY CLEAN & WASHABLE: تصميم عملي وسهل التنظيف، تقدر تغسل الفازة والورد تحت المية مباشرة وينشف في دقايق ويرجع جديد بلمعته الأصلية.`

- **Audit Status**: **[UNSAFE TO PUBLISH]** 🛑
- **Physical Reality & Failure Point**:
  - Photographic inspection of the pot opening (`hero_white_isolated.jpg` and `packaging.jpeg`) reveals that the artificial stems are bedded in decorative granular filler (sand, gravel, or crushed stone composite).
  - Submerging or washing the pot directly under running water will cause:
    1. Granular filler displacement or dissolution.
    2. Water pooling inside the pot with no drainage hole, causing mold, trapped moisture, or odor.
    3. Potential detachment of the metallic gold foil band under hot water or detergent.
- **Corrective Rewrite**:
  - Replace claim with:
  ```text
  EASY CARE & DUST-RESISTANT: تصميم عملي لا يحتاج إلى ماء أو شمس، يُنظف بسهولة بمسحة خفيفة بقطعة قماش ناعمة مبللة أو مجفف هواء بارد لإزالة الأتربة خلال ثوانٍ.
  ```
